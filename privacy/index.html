Spark Standby is operated by Seif Sharara, a sole proprietor. This Privacy Policy explains how Seif Sharara ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with this website and related business communications.

1. Information We Collect

Information we collect may include:

  • Name, including first name
  • Email address
  • Phone or mobile phone number
  • Form submissions and support/contact communications you voluntarily provide
  • Basic website usage information, if analytics tools are added in the future

This website does not currently use analytics tools. This policy will be updated if that changes.

The live demo form is embedded on this website. The external demo messaging workflow still requires end-to-end verification. The browser-based homepage simulation collects no personal information and sends no calls or texts.

When the live demo is available, information processed for it may include your first name, mobile phone number, form submission, SMS consent status, inbound demo call records, and demo SMS conversation data. Consent date, time, and source are retained where recorded by the form/communications platform.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries

  • Schedule appointments and communicate with customers
  • Send SMS communications when consent has been provided

3. SMS Privacy

If you consent to receive SMS messages from Spark Standby, operated by Seif Sharara, your mobile number and consent information are used for the messaging purposes disclosed at consent.

Mobile information and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process this information only as necessary to deliver communications and operate the service, including hosting, customer support, and consent verification.

Providing a phone number alone is not SMS consent. SMS consent is collected separately through an optional, unchecked checkbox. You can withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP. The live-demo form is available at /demo/.

Third-party vendors may process information only as necessary to provide services such as website hosting, communications, customer relationship management, or other business operations. We do not name specific vendors here unless necessary for transparency.

7. Your Choices
