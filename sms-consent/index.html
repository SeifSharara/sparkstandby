How SMS consent is collected

The following describes the web-form flow being prepared for the requested non-marketing live demo. Visit the live demo opt-in page. Its form placeholder does not collect consent.

  1. Visit /demo/ and enter your first name and mobile phone number in the connected form once available.
  2. Choose whether to select a separate, optional SMS consent checkbox. It is unchecked by default. Providing a phone number alone does not provide SMS consent.
  3. Submit the form. Consent is recorded only if you affirmatively opt in. Successful submissions, including those without SMS consent, lead to /demo/ready/.
  4. Call the live demo number, (571) 556-5051, from the same mobile number you submitted. The call intentionally goes unanswered.
  5. The missed call may trigger the requested demo SMS only after prior opt-in has been recorded and verified. Without opt-in, no demo SMS is sent.

Messaging terms

Consent applies to the requested live-demo conversation, not marketing or promotional messages. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or form submission.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of further SMS messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance or support information.

STOP and HELP handling must be enabled and tested before the messaging program launches. For support now, email seifsharara@gmail.com or call the general contact number (703) 678-1815.

A demonstration, not a service appointment

Spark Standby is not acting as an HVAC contractor in this demo. No technician is dispatched, no equipment diagnosis is provided, and no real appointment is scheduled.
